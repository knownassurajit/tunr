Privacy Policy for Tunr

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 · Last Updated: October 21, 2026
Application: Tunr (com.knownassurajit.tunr_tool.app)
Developer: Surajit (@knownassurajit)

1. Introduction

Tunr is designed with a strict privacy-first, local-only architecture. Tunr does not track, store, profile, or sell any user information.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account, email, name, phone number, or login credentials
  • No telemetry, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or trackers
  • No location data
  • No access to personal files, media, contacts, or documents

3. Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used only to analyze live instrument audio in RAM with the McLeod Pitch Method. Audio is never recorded, written to disk, cached, or transmitted. Buffers are discarded after each analysis frame.

4. Vibration (VIBRATE)

Used only for in-tune and metronome haptic pulses. It can be turned off in Settings.

5. Network

Tunr does not declare android.permission.INTERNET. It operates fully offline and cannot send data off-device.

6. Children

Tunr does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
